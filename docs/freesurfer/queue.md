# FreeSurfer 8.2.0: the queue

**Closed to FreeSurfer clean-room implementers** (it names FreeSurfer's files and functions;
`docs/freesurfer/README.md`). A clean-room implementer gets its item through its brief.

The order of work for FreeSurfer's port ([the plan](../freesurfer-plan.md), "Working modes"):
written by the inventory (relay 6), kept by the orchestrator. The waves, the modules and the
crates are explained in [waves.md](waves.md); each program's facts are on its card
(`cards/<program>.md`). The tables below are generated by `waves.py` (waves.md §7); the
orchestrator marks an item's progress in its brief and in NOTES, not by editing a generated row.

## How to take an item

- **One item, one relay.** An item is bounded: one module's files (or a piece of them), one
  program, or one piece of a large program, at most about **2,500 lines** of FreeSurfer's function
  bodies (the budget is an assumption: calibrate it on the first three items). *Lines* are what
  the item brings: the functions only it reaches and its own files, counted once in the whole
  queue (static reach, so upper bounds: waves.md §3).
- **Kinds.** `replica` (a module of `crates-freesurfer/`) and a program's kind (`c++`, `tcsh`,
  `python-network`, ...): a **replica item**, whose author is also the **spec writer** of what it
  ports (`specs/freesurfer-<module>.md` or `specs/<program>.md`; one reading of the source, two
  products); `permissive`: a third-party piece ported from its own upstream into the main package
  (no FreeSurfer source); `spec + clean room`: a spec writer, then a clean-room implementer;
  `clean room only`: Washington University's 4dfp tools (P-12); `completeness`: the option paths
  of earlier programs into a module just brought; `plumbing`, `oracle`, `inventory + spec`: what
  their row says.
- **Every program item, in full** (the plan, "Each program's validation"): every option, message,
  exit status and failure case with D13's switches; its spec; the replay on every recorded head in
  both streams (its *Checks*); synthetic edge cases for every option and failure; golden cases
  (volumes up to 32³, small synthetic surfaces); its defects decided by rules A to D (FreeSurfer's
  defect IDs from the orchestrator's range); its record; memory near the original's; its pages.
  *Checks* names what the recordings give it: "replay: N recorded steps in R runs" (the program is
  a recorded step), "through ...'s recorded steps" (it runs inside a script's step: the replay
  runs the script's step, or the program with the arguments the script's log shows), or
  fMRIPrep's and sMRIPrep's calls on the recorded subjects (programs.md §5; new runs through the
  oracle-output cache at one thread).
- **Inputs** names the modules the item uses (they come before it), the programs a script runs,
  the data files the program reads (data-files.toml) and what it *waits for* (a later module its
  option paths reach: completed by that wave's "option paths" item).
- **Side by side.** In a normal week one agent takes the next item. In a burst: items of different
  modules or programs (never two agents on one module); the N track beside 3.2-3.5 once 3.1's
  formats exist; **the clean rooms one wave behind** (the table after each wave's: the clean rooms
  of the previous wave, from the specs its replica items wrote, by agents who never read
  FreeSurfer's source).
- **Before 3.1:** the preparation's remaining sub-tasks (the recorder and the replay tool, the
  restricted paths for the clean-room audit, the handoff: NOTES of the preparation part).
