## Spec questions (for the spec writer; specs/topup.md)

- §4.6, FSL's rotation derivatives of the movements (findings 14): its translations' gradient
  agrees with the cost's derivative, its rotations' does not (exact for linear images, 0.4 to 26 %
  off on real ones; a zero-derivative translation gets −7e-7); what does FSL compute? The original
  uses the exact derivative; 9 record cases with unconverged movements are expected divergences
  for it, and 4,462 random cases. Defect or deliberate approximation?
- §4.4, the refit between levels: the penalty is `0.005²` times the squares of only the two
  second differences that wrap around the ends (`c[n-1] − 2c[0] + c[1]`, `c[n-2] − 2c[n-1] +
  c[0]`), from probes F, G, H (findings 11); which rows does the spec mean? Also the refit when
  warpres changes at the same subsampling; the intermediate spacing round(previous warpres / new
  voxel).
- §4.6, the quadratic splines' bending energy where a knot falls on a voxel (even knot spacing):
  FSL's Levenberg-Marquardt Hessian's regulariser part differs from the original's by up to
  3.4 % (`--debug=3`, case 10753: the data part equal to 2e-7) while the first gradient and cost
  agree; the outer piece, the inner, either limit, their average, the exact integral were tried,
  none is FSL's (47 random cases open, findings 36).
- §7.4, `lsr` on lines whose length divides 2^32 (16 voxels): 8 random cases differ, the restored
  image's 99th percentile 1.4e-3 to 0.13 (reproducer 8089).
- FSL-133 (findings 21, probes probe133: one bright voxel at 0, 1, 15, 29 of a 30-voxel line):
  what FSL reads at the wrap-around of lines whose length does not divide 2^32 (the register's

- FSL-134 (findings 22, probe134): how FSL lays a field of the images' voxel count but another
